## 2025-05-18 - Avoid converting Float32Array to JS Array in vector search loops
**Learning:** In the SQLite-based `MemoryVectorStore`, vectors are stored as binary BLOBs and decoded into `Float32Array`. Calling `Array.from(float32Array)` converts typed arrays to standard JS arrays on every row in the search loop (and was redundantly called twice per candidate during cosine similarity calculation). For high-dimensional embeddings (e.g. 1536 floats), this introduces massive allocation and garbage collection overhead (~12x slowdown on 1000 items). `cosineSimilarity` and filtering can operate directly on `Float32Array` indexed elements without converting to generic arrays, and operations like `keywordSearch()` and `list()` don't need to instantiate vector arrays at all.
**Action:** When implementing or optimizing vector stores and math utilities in TypeScript, keep vectors as `Float32Array` or accept `number[] | Float32Array` across calculation utilities to avoid array materialization on hot query paths.
